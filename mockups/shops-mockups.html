Where to Find Native Seeds & Plants — Upstate New York

Nurseries, seed exchanges, library seed collections and the once-a-year sales that supply most of the region's native stock. Free and local-ecotype sources are marked.

Western New York Erie · Niagara · Monroe · Genesee · Orleans · Wyoming

Bare-root conservation stock dominates here — the county Soil & Water districts are by far the cheapest route to canopy and shrub layers, but they sell once a year on a January-to-March order window. Potted herb-layer natives mostly come from the spring chapter sales.

Monroe County SWCD Seedling Sale
Rochester · Monroe Co.
~32 species of bare-root trees and shrubs in bundles of 10–25. Order Jan–Mar, pick up April.
