After dragging task 5 down to position 7.

E — Row moved, and what it says Dragging renumbers, and predecessors stay with the position, not the task. Slot 5 keeps 3FS,4FS whatever now sits in it. The notice spells out every row that changed hands.
  • The strip never shifts the page. It occupies a fixed 56px whether it has something to say or not — compare where the table starts in state A against here. Only the contents fade in.
  • Undo is in the strip, because a reorder that rewires dependencies is exactly the edit you want to take back.
  • This is not MS Project's rule — MSP carries links with the task. Noted on export.
