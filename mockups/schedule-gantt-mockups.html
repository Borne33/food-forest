One band per planting phase. The forest grows down the page.

Mockup 3 — Phase bands Drops the single continuous grid for a band per phase, each with its own mini-Gantt across the season.
  • Matches how the plan is actually authored — the Plants tab already thinks in phases.
  • Seasonal shading is the star: each band shows its planting window, so "too late for bare root" is obvious.
  • Cross-phase dependencies are the weak point — an arrow between bands is easy to miss.

What happens in which month, year by year.

Mockup 4 — Season calendar Not a Gantt. A year × month matrix with tasks as blocks in month cells — the schedule as a planting calendar.
  • Answers "what do I do in April" better than any Gantt does.
  • Seasonality is the axis, so planting windows are structural rather than shading.
  • Loses day-level precision, dependency arrows and the critical path — it cannot replace the Gantt, it can only sit alongside it.
